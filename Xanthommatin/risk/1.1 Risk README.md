# Risk & Strategic Evaluation Module (`risk/`)

## 1. Overview
The `risk/` directory houses executive decision metrics and strategic modeling tools for Xanthommatin[cite: 2]. Designed as a high-stakes survival and planning layer, this module bridges raw financial telemetry with decisive operational actions[cite: 2].

---

## 2. The Walk-Away Matrix (`walk_away.py`)
The **Walk-Away Matrix** calculates an immediate, unvarnished valuation of pulling up stumps (quitting or liquidating) in real time[cite: 4]. It strips away emotional projections and sunk-cost fallacies, giving the CEO an objective snapshot of terminal reality[cite: 4].

### Core Metrics:
* **Assets (A):** Real-time cash reserves plus immediate short-term receivables[cite: 4].
* **Liabilities (L):** Current accounts payable combined with mandatory wind-down overhead (severance, final legal fees, contract break penalties)[cite: 4].
* **Net Terminal Position (NTLV):** A - L (The cash remaining the moment the doors lock)[cite: 4].
* **Runway Delta:** Comparison of ongoing monthly burn against immediate liquidation cash[cite: 4].

---

## 3. Strategic Ablation in Business
Adapting quantitative machine learning principles (feature ablation) to executive strategy, **structural ablation** measures the business impact of removing a variable (e.g., cutting a product line, slashing contractor overhead, or divesting a division)[cite: 2]. 

By simulating the delta in monthly burn and wind-down liability when a specific operational weight is dropped, executives can test whether restructuring buys meaningful survival runway or merely delays an inevitable deficit exit[cite: 2].
